I am a dual-degree undergraduate student pursuing a **Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Data Science & Applications from the Indian Institute of Technology, Madras** and a **Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Electrical Engineering from Bundelkhand Institute of Engineering & Technology (BIET), Jhansi**.

I am honored to be recognized as an **Aspire Institute Grantee** (initiative founded by Harvard Business School faculty) and a **Reliance Foundation Undergraduate STEM Scholar** (selected among the top 5,000 STEM students nationwide from over 1,000,000+ applicants).

---

## Academic & Research Interests

My academic interests center on the intersection of **statistical data science, computational astrophysics, quantitative modeling, and resilient distributed systems**:

* **Computational Astrophysics & Survey Science:** Developing deep learning ensembles and gradient-boosted models for photometric redshift estimation of Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN), and analyzing 3D space velocities and dynamical mass segregation in open star clusters using Gaia DR3 astrometric catalogs.
* **Quantitative Finance & Risk Engineering:** Modeling multi-asset portfolio risk through high-iteration Monte Carlo simulations, Markowitz Efficient Frontier optimization, and dynamic parametric/conditional Value-at-Risk (VaR/CVaR) computation.
* **Low-Bandwidth Mobile & Distributed Systems:** Architecting ultra-low-bandwidth, low-latency live study ecosystems tailored for tier-2/3 towns and rural students facing fragile 2G/3G connectivity (funded by an international seed grant from the Aspire Institute).
* **Trustworthy AI & Responsible Machine Learning:** Auditing algorithmic bias, data quality, feature attribution, and explainability frameworks (SHAP, LIME).
